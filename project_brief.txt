Portfolio Project Brief: Customer Support Knowledge Base

Refund policy:
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase if they provide proof of purchase.
Digital products that have been downloaded may not be refundable unless required by applicable law.

Shipping:
Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days.
Customers receive a tracking link after the order is dispatched.

Account security:
Never ask a customer to share their password or one-time passcode. If a customer cannot access their account, direct them to the official password reset flow.

Support hours:
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local business time.
